# Let Me Cook

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Keep Let Me Cook a cooking product. Help people find recipes, choose dinners, prepare a shopping list and follow a recipe with local AI assistance.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing React client uses the Java gateway, local Supabase and local AI worker. Preserve authentication, recipe sources, preference constraints, editable plans, kitchen records, cooking sessions and explicit audio controls. Audio and countdowns are browser features; reliable locked-screen alarms are not implemented. Public creator publication remains feature gated.

## Brand Commitments

The user has explicitly requested English product copy and preservation of the existing Sunny mascot, yellow/cream theme and visual identity. The latest requirement covers every route and subpage: left and right panels paired in the same row must have matching visual heights, and the interface must be coherent and suitable for a commercial website.

## Evidence on Hand

Original recipe photos are stored locally. Existing implemented features and acceptance evidence are recorded in docs/ and the release reports. Do not invent customers, testimonials, paid plans, cloud integrations or production certification.

## Product Principles

- Keep user-facing copy short and useful.
- Preserve real content, source attribution and working controls.
- Give the primary action an obvious place.
- Keep the same workflow usable on desktop, tablet and phone.
- Public marketing explains benefits and features, with Home as a compact introduction, Features as the complete experience and How it works as practical guidance. Keep architecture and model names out of those pages.
- The authorized marketing presentation includes AI feature ideas without Coming soon badges. Descriptive product vision and clearly named examples do not establish operational availability; link actions to their actual destination.
- Extend the current website and its existing content. A feature finder and short kitchen guides help people reach the working tools; preserve the recipe catalog, account records and current routes when adding content.

## Open Decisions

Commercial licensing, public hosting, business eligibility and a representative human pilot remain separate decisions. This visual redesign does not resolve them.
