# LetMeCook local kitchen release — 8 October 2026

This release implements a connected local cooking workflow: confirmed pantry entries, real recipe planning, measured shopping coverage, source-grounded cooking questions and confirmed stock consumption. It is a technical release for local testing. It does not establish customer traction, food-waste reduction, commercial content rights or successful human pilot results.

The default evidence release label is `2026.10.08-kitchen`. Record the actual Git revision and any uncommitted changes alongside an evaluation; the label alone does not identify an immutable software build.

## Implemented capabilities and their limits

| Capability | Current behavior | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sunny text and photo search | English/Vietnamese requests parsed locally; photo ingredients must be reviewed and confirmed; SQL hard filters precede semantic ranking | Photos do not supply verified amounts, freshness, dates or allergen safety |
| Recipe catalog | Real source recipes, original local images and source/photo attribution; pagination, highest-rated sorting and minimum overall rating | Overall averages come only from submitted `overall` review ratings; empty ratings are not invented |
| Pantry | Explicitly confirmed named lots, known/unknown amounts, units and user-entered dates; personal or accepted household scope; change ledger | Exact names and reviewed aliases only; unknown stock does not count toward measured coverage |
| Dinner planner | 1, 3 or 7 unique dinners; servings 1–12; maximum cooking time 5–240 minutes; source-labelled diets, known ingredient contradictions, exclusions and disliked recipes enforced | Partial or empty results carry warnings; no silent relaxation or global-optimum claim |
| Must-use | Exact ingredients used in every selected meal (`perMeal`) or at least once in the selected plan (`plan`), with a positive allocation from measured pantry stock | Ingredient presence alone is insufficient; stock is shared once, and unknown or incompatible quantities cannot satisfy it |
| Shopping and budget | Fractions and known source servings scaled conservatively; compatible unit spellings consolidated; full-pack purchase rounding; quote ID, area, source, observation date and currency preserved | No guessed density or unit conversion; unknown amounts/prices or mixed currencies cannot verify a budget |
| What-if and swaps | Explicit deterministic What-if produces a new preview using verified original conditions; Apply updates the draft; canonical swaps/refetches use current authoritative profile and stock | Same-selection recalculation still rejects invalid swaps; no LLM rerun or silent relaxation; saving remains explicit |
| Cook-along | Saved immutable recipe steps, current step, timers and bounded conversation; source citations returned verbatim | Supported answers must be a passage from a cited step; unsupported temperatures, amounts, substitutions and safety claims are refused |
| Local voice | Explicit microphone recording; local Whisper transcription, editable transcript; local eSpeak NG WAV playback | CPU speech models; synthetic diagnostics are not field accuracy; speech is not a cloud browser engine |
| Completion | User confirms actual lot quantities; version checks, scope revision and idempotent transactional confirmation update stock, ledger and an authorized saved meal slot persisted in the session | A shopping checkbox or recipe view never deducts stock or counts as cooking; an edited saved recipe cannot be confirmed through its old session |
| Households | Accepted invitations, owner/editor/viewer roles, shared pantry and shopping; stale updates rejected | A caller-supplied user ID never grants access; an invitation code is not an email delivery service |
| Creator tools | Workspace settings, permission declarations and administrator-approved ingredient substitution previews; new rules remain pending until review | Administrator curation does not attest expert credentials; a declaration or reviewed ratio is not independent rights verification or a certified dietary/cooking guarantee |
| Evidence | Opt-in confirmations, consent revocation/deletion/export, retained 90-day activity and explicitly named enrollment with a fixed days-22–28 window | The selected-household rate is not an aggregate pilot cohort; immature, withdrawn, incomplete or expired observations have no measured rate |
| Operations | Trusted admin catalog declarations, reviewed aliases, separate public-demo/kitchen/voice/collaboration flags, contact inbox and local newsletter capture | Mailpit captures messages locally; it is not proof of external email delivery or paid conversions |

Planner stock coverage checks each dinner date. Earlier-expiring lots can supply valid earlier meals; their quantities never supply meals after the recorded date. A partial usable lot is shared across eligible measured demands, so per-meal positive must-use allocations remain possible without spending stock twice. Recorded future purchase dates cannot supply earlier meals. Missing dates remain visible and require the cook to assess condition; the system does not infer freshness.

Vegetarian/vegan searches, recommendations and planning still require explicit source diet labels, and also reject known contradictions in the listed ingredients. A vegetarian-labelled recipe containing Parma ham is excluded; vegan filtering also checks known dairy, egg and bee products. Explicit plant alternatives and named ingredients such as coconut milk, peanut butter and oyster mushrooms are handled locally to each occurrence: a phrase such as “vegan cheese and ham” or “oat milk or regular milk” remains contradictory. Shared worker/Java patterns run before ranking and pagination; substitution previews check both the replacement and the resulting list against saved vegetarian/vegan preferences. These diet checks are separate from user-entered medical/allergen exclusions and never create a synthetic allergy. They do not infer unlabelled diets or certify hidden ingredients or cross-contact.

The planner checks at most 300 ranked candidates when enforcing must-use or budget. Its deterministic search can return a partial plan even when a different combination might exist. It never reports an unpriced plan as within budget. Alternatives are checked as valid swaps for at least one selected slot; recalculation checks the specific requested swap again.

Saved-plan load rebuilds current canonical selections. If an owned saved row is no longer valid, the response contains only its ID, version, week and a regeneration message; it does not expose old recipe, shopping, intent or profile snapshots. A newly generated replacement retains that existing week's version for compare-and-set saving. Authentication, upstream failures and busy responses are still errors rather than invalid-plan recovery.

A cooking session started with an authorized saved meal slot stores that association and the server-derived saved-plan version. Resume keeps it without relying on the browser URL. Completion refetches the current owned plan and selected scope, verifies the recipe still matches, and permits one receipt per saved slot. A conflicting caller binding is rejected; the same completed operation remains idempotently replayable.

## API and trust boundary

Use the web origin `http://localhost:9401` for the UI and its `/api` proxy. The backend is loopback `127.0.0.1:9402`; the worker is loopback `127.0.0.1:9501`; Ollama is loopback `127.0.0.1:11434`. Supabase API, database and Studio use `56421`, `56422` and `56423`; Mailpit SMTP/UI use `56425`/`56426`.

- Guest-capable: recipe catalog reads, `/api/ai/status`, `/api/ai/search`, `/api/ai/vision`, `/api/meal-plans/generate`, `/api/meal-plans/recalculate` and `/api/meal-plans/what-if` without pantry context.
- Authenticated: plan save/load/preferences and all `/api/kitchen/*` operations. The verified JWT subject supplies identity; the gateway derives the current profile and accepted household scope.
- Trusted admin: `/api/platform/admin/*` and application cache management. Admin authority comes from server-managed `app_metadata.role`, not user-editable metadata.
- Worker routes are internal trusted-context APIs. Do not expose port 9501 publicly or let browser callers submit profile, pantry or price-book snapshots directly.

A planner request adds these bounded settings to the existing request:

```json
{
  "weekStart": "2026-10-12",
  "servings": 2,
  "maxCookTime": 45,
  "prompt": "",
  "dietaryPreferences": [],
  "excludedIngredients": [],
  "useProfile": true,
  "usePantry": true,
  "householdId": null,
  "mealCount": 3,
  "mustUseIngredients": ["rice"],
  "mustUseScope": "plan",
  "budget": {"amount": 20, "currency": "CAD"}
}
```

Must-use has at most 10 exact names. Budget is optional, positive, at most 1,000,000 and has at most two decimal places; a valid currency is checked at the gateway. Must-use and budget require authenticated measured pantry context. The response includes `pantryCoverage`, `mustUseStatus` and `budgetStatus` alongside canonical meals and shopping items.

Recalculation/save sends `intent` unchanged, `meals: [{dayIndex, recipeId}]` and valid `checkedItems`. HMAC metadata `_parsed`, `_requirements` and `_signature` binds the original prompt and hard requirements. Recalculation may tighten requirements; it cannot erase initial exclusions/must-use or raise the original budget. Legitimate legacy signatures without `_requirements` can be upgraded; stripping `_requirements` from a new signature fails. A new save uses `version: 0`; subsequent saves require the returned version and stale writes return 409.

`POST /api/meal-plans/what-if` accepts the same serialized plan with changed settings and returns a plain canonical replacement preview. It does not save or apply changes and does not rerun the language model. Original time/exclusions/must-use/budget remain enforced. `POST /api/meal-plans/refresh-prices` is authenticated and explicitly refreshes the selected price observations for the same meals; changed prices must still meet the original budget.

Price observations are frozen in signed `intent._pricing` references with ID, version and a digest of the actual quote/provenance. Recalculate, save, load and What-if retain those selections. Stock refresh returns the same canonical planner coverage with current stock, rather than running a second calculation against an unpinned price book. It requires the plan's original personal/household scope and rejects a stock revision that changes during the check. Newly added quotes do not silently reprice a plan. A missing/changed used quote produces an error until the user deliberately refreshes and reviews the result. Legitimate older signatures without `_pricing` upgrade once; a new signature cannot be weakened by stripping the metadata. Preserve the complete signed `intent` object.

## Models and local provisioning

Runtime inference uses existing pretrained models; there is no LetMeCook-specific fine-tuning:

| Task | Runtime |
| --- | --- |
| Text parsing and cook evidence selection | Ollama `qwen3:8b`, Q4_K_M in the observed deployment |
| Ingredient vision | Ollama `qwen3-vl:4b-instruct`, Q4_K_M in the observed deployment |
| Active recommendation | `qwen3-embedding:0.6b`, pinned digest, 1,024-dimensional hybrid dense/BM25 index with RRF and MMR |
| Legacy compatibility ranking | `all-MiniLM-L6-v2`, pinned revision, local CPU weights and immutable 384-dimensional cache |
| Speech recognition | `faster-whisper==1.2.1`, multilingual base, CPU `int8` |
| Speech synthesis | eSpeak NG, CPU formant synthesis; observed version 1.52.0 |

The worker uses one process and a bounded single inference lane. Capacity is four active-plus-waiting jobs, the wait bound is 25 seconds, and token admission is bounded. Cook requests precede queued voice/text/vision/planner model work; an active request is not preempted. Busy admission returns 429. Model or dependency failures produce explicit unavailable/error responses. These are bounds, not a production concurrency or latency SLA.

Run from the repository root on the existing local installation:

```bash
bash deploy/local-up.sh
```

Prerequisites are Docker Compose, Supabase CLI, Python 3, curl and the existing loopback Ollama service. The script provisions missing text/vision/embedding models, applies schema/data and additive migrations, preserves protected local configuration, provisions separate gateway/worker database identities and builds the services. It validates the existing recommendation generation before waiting for semantic readiness; restarting does not rebuild a valid index. Preserve `deploy/.env.local` and `deploy/.env.roles`: they contain runtime credentials and the persistent planner signing key. Never commit or display them.

On a fresh model volume, explicitly build the active hybrid index after the services have been built, then repeat `local-up.sh`:

```bash
docker compose --env-file deploy/.env.local -f deploy/compose.yaml run --rm --no-deps recommendation python -m src.cache_bootstrap --build-hybrid --expected-count 10000
```

The expected count is for this installation's imported catalog; choose the actual public catalog count on another installation. Model digest mismatches fail before publication. For an older installation with only flat SBERT files, explicitly use `--migrate-legacy --expected-count 10000`; migration preserves original files and uses no model download. Pinned SBERT weights and a legacy rebuild are separate explicit operator actions described in `RecommendationSystem/README-cache-generation.txt`.

Runtime roles own no database objects. The worker is read-only and RLS limits it to current public/demo-visible catalog data. The gateway keeps trusted authorization checks and BYPASSRLS for its existing internal writes, but cannot create databases, roles or public-schema objects. Schema changes and role provisioning use the local operator identity. `/api/health/live` confirms only the process; `/api/health/ready` checks database/schema; `/api/health/ai?capability=recommendation|text|vision|voice|speech|catalog` checks each optional capability independently.

For a fresh speech installation, explicitly provision the model into the recommendation model volume after building the worker:

```bash
docker compose --env-file deploy/.env.local -f deploy/compose.yaml run --rm --no-deps recommendation python -c "from faster_whisper.utils import download_model; download_model('base', output_dir='/app/model/voice/faster-whisper-base')"
```

The default speech path is `/app/model/voice/faster-whisper-base`, configurable using `LOCAL_STT_MODEL_PATH`. Speech requests never download weights. Confirm `/api/ai/status` or worker `/ai/voice/status` shows `stt.status: ready` and `tts.status: ready` before the voice demo. Model provisioning may require network access; runtime inference stays local. Ollama GPU use is verified through `/api/ps` and reported VRAM, rather than inferred from a container's device list. Browser connectivity and ordinary web resources mean this is not an offline-phone product.

## Catalog rights and public demo gate

The 10,000-recipe/original-image collection is a private technical test catalog, not a licensed commercial catalog. Source metadata retains original attribution and rights-reserved notices. Local download and source links do not establish redistribution permission. Code distribution rights also require review.

When `publicDemo` is enabled, public reads and recommendation/planning paths require an admin-approved recipe permission declaration with a nonblank note and an original local image. This gate is software enforcement of reviewed declarations, not independent verification that the declaration is correct. Do not mark imported rights-reserved records approved simply to populate a public demo. A public demo must use team-authored content or content for which actual recipe and original-photo rights have been obtained and documented.

The proposed human pilot still requires content review, recruitment, consent materials, task protocol and participant observation. Startup-program acceptance, willingness to pay, payment/revenue, food-waste change and time savings remain external work. An empty consent dashboard is an honest initial state.

For the staged owner-only pilot, keep `collaborationEnabled: false` while enabling only reviewed required modules. Accepted household membership remains checked; editor writes/invitations require the collaboration gate to open. Named cohort enrollment is an explicit confirmed household-owner action with current evidence consent, a server-recorded enrollment timestamp, release/consent versions and a fixed observation window. It does not backdate recruitment or create a matured household merely because historical events exist. The dashboard distinguishes this fixed cohort from its older retained-activity view.

## Verification and reproducible evidence

`deploy/evaluate-local.py` performs read-only readiness checks by default. `--inference` also submits labelled synthetic cook questions and bounded concurrency bursts; these do not create product records or human pilot evidence:

```bash
python3 deploy/evaluate-local.py --output work/evaluation/readiness.json
python3 deploy/evaluate-local.py --inference --output work/evaluation/local-inference.json
python3 deploy/evaluate-corpus.py --output work/evaluation/quantity-corpus.json
python3 deploy/evaluate-model-swap.py --image /path/to/permitted-original-photo.jpg --output work/evaluation/model-swap.json
```

Review the report's overall `passed` field and each check. Source code being present is not proof that the currently running containers contain it. Repeat evaluation after the final build and record model digests, queue status and runtime GPU data. A small authored synthetic check is a regression diagnostic, not accuracy on a representative held-out cooking benchmark or a 100-request p95 latency study.

The recorded `local-evaluation-final.json` passed overall: all seven frozen cook cases passed, and bursts at concurrency 1/3/5 returned only 200/429 with a largest sampled response of 2.784 seconds. Supported answers used exact source passages/citations; absent temperatures, new ingredients, compound safety questions and injection cases were refused. These are bounded regression observations, not a household load test or a latency guarantee.

The recorded `model-swap-evaluation-instruct.json` also passed overall. Initial text took 0.918 seconds, the original-photo vision call 7.426 seconds, warm vision 1.369 seconds, the synthetic blank holdout 0.651 seconds and text after vision 5.823 seconds. Ollama reported vision GPU residency of 4,960,301,952 VRAM bytes and text residency of 6,614,028,416 bytes at the relevant stages. The original photo had no human-labelled ingredient ground truth: its pass establishes response/schema/model-switch behavior, not ingredient accuracy. The blank holdout returned the expected empty observations. Earlier `qwen3-vl:4b` attempts exhausted the thinking/output budget and returned 502; those failures are retained. The working default is `qwen3-vl:4b-instruct` with the bounded image request configuration, not a claim that every model variant works.

The speech diagnostic was a synthetic eSpeak-to-Whisper round trip: English word error rate 0.50 and Vietnamese 0.8571 on one short sample each. It confirms real local audio processing but does not justify a high-quality Vietnamese speech claim. Keep transcripts editable, retain typed input and conduct a human speech evaluation before relying on hands-free use.

## Backlog acceptance not established by this release

Working modules do not automatically close every acceptance statement in the original 44-task plan:

- **E04/E17/E22 evaluation depth:** the frozen authored corpus and source tests are small technical diagnostics. A representative, rights-reviewed intent/photo/planner/question corpus with human-labelled outcomes still needs expansion. A paired household manual baseline requires actual participants and cannot be simulated into pilot evidence.
- **E25B measured operation:** use the actual text/vision sequence report to assess cold/warm/swap/queue timing after the final deploy. A small run does not establish voice/cook contention under household load or a production latency guarantee.

External rights, interviews, recruitment, the reviewed pilot catalog, four-week observation, expert substitution review and B2B demand evidence remain external acceptance work. Administrator-approved substitutions and workspace customization do not prove X02 expert credentials or X03 market demand.

Backup/restore is separate from readiness:

```bash
bash deploy/backup-local.sh work/backups/release-2026-10-08
bash deploy/verify-restore-local.sh work/backups/release-2026-10-08
```

The restore verifier uses a temporary database and byte-verifies extracted original images/storage uploads, RLS/ACLs and the runtime-key checksum without replacing live data. Keep the backup directory private: it contains auth/application data and runtime secrets. The observed restore log reported snapshot-count, asset-byte, permissions and runtime-key verification; re-run after relevant data/migration changes.
