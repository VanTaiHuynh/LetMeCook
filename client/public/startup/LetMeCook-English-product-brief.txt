LetMeCook — English MVP product brief
Proposed features and targets; no pilot results are claimed.


VISION

Try Sunny today, then explore our plan for a kitchen that helps you choose a meal, buy what is missing and cook it through.
For busy households of 2–4 people who cook at home regularly. The pilot region and participant group are still to be selected.
A pantry you can trust, a plan that respects your needs and support while cooking could make dinner easier to decide and worth coming back for.
Less effort choosing dinner: Start with your ingredients and today's needs, then see a few options with clear reasons.
Buy what is missing: See what you have, what you need and what still needs checking before you shop.
Cook through to the last step: Follow the recipe, confirm the meal and record what you used for next time.

SIX PLANNED FEATURES

F01 — A real pantry
Take a photo, correct the ingredients and keep an editable pantry. Add amounts and dates yourself, and choose what you want to use first.
Available ingredients help suggest meals. You choose separately what must be used in every meal or in at least one meal of a plan. Photos cannot tell us amounts, freshness or expiry dates; unknown details stay visible.
Available now: photo recognition with ingredient review and confirmation.
Planned: a saved pantry with editable amounts, units and dates.

F02 — A plan for three dinners
Plan dinners around the number of people, your time and food preferences. Prioritize what needs using and see what is missing.
The plan would account for what each meal uses, so the same stock is not counted twice. Shopping amounts need clear quantities and compatible units. A budget can only be checked with suitable prices, pack sizes and complete price coverage.
Available now: recipe search, filters and ranking.
Planned: start the pilot with one meal. Add three meals and priced budgets after the required checks pass.

F03 — What-if and clear explanations
Have 15 minutes instead of 25? Feeding one more person? Compare the meals, portions and shopping list.
See the needs we understood, what you have, what is missing and what is unknown. Exclusions stay in place. If no option fits, you decide whether to change your request.
Available now: understood search conditions, match reasons and recipe sources.
Planned: compare meal plans when you change one requirement.

F04 — Sunny Cook-along
Keep your place, set timers and ask about the recipe you chose. Check the source behind the answer.
Resume your cooking step after reloading. Questions beyond the recipe would be marked as unknown; AI would not invent amounts or temperatures, or judge whether food is cooked from a photo. Confirm what you used before your pantry is updated.
Available now: recipe instructions.
Planned: saved cooking steps, timers, answers grounded in the recipe and confirmed meal completion.

F05 — A shared household kitchen
Share a pantry and shopping list with people in your household. Choosing dinner and buying ingredients can follow the same plan.
People accept an invitation before getting access. Simultaneous changes would be checked, and retrying an action would not use the same ingredients twice. Recording a cooked meal always needs your confirmation.
Available now: individual accounts and recipe management.
Planned: household members, shared shopping and a record of pantry changes. Sharing opens after its release checks pass.

F06 — Evidence Console
Track confirmed meals, returning households and task results. Improve the product from what people find useful.
Figures would show the trial group, observation period and totals behind each percentage. Opening a recipe does not count as cooking it. Using ingredients near a chosen date is a signal; actual waste reduction or savings need separate measurements.
Available now: AI status and technical evaluation results.
Planned: a pilot dashboard using consented activity, without raw photos or requests in analytics.

LOCAL AI

Sunny combines local AI with recipe data and explicit search conditions. We still need to measure how much it helps people choose and cook dinner.
Models on a local server: Sunny uses pretrained models to understand text and photos and help rank recipes. These are existing models, not proprietary models trained by LetMeCook.
Local processing for Sunny requests: Sunny's inference flow processes requests and ingredient photos on the local server without sending them to a cloud AI service. Recognition photos are temporary, and you confirm the ingredients.
Real sources and clear unknowns: Sunny searches the recipe catalog. Missing information stays visible, and unmet requirements should be reported. Ingredient detection and exclusions do not certify allergy safety.
Your browser needs a connection to the local server. A fully offline phone experience is not implemented.

PROPOSED 12-WEEK ROADMAP

The plan assumes two full-time engineers and a founder handling research and content. Each stage has release checks; pilot timing also depends on recruitment.
Weeks 01–02 — Understand the problem
Interview 15 home cooks, clarify code and content rights, and prepare 20 demo recipes with original photos.

Weeks 03–04 — Build the pantry
Add confirmed pantry entries, prepare 100 reviewed pilot recipes and test ingredient entry.

Weeks 05–06 — Choose one dinner
Plan a single meal, compare changed requirements and build a shopping list with unknown amounts clearly marked.

Weeks 07–08 — Complete the cooking loop
Add cooking guidance, questions about the selected recipe and meal confirmation. Check permissions, privacy and readiness before onboarding.

Weeks 09–10 — Try it in real kitchens
Begin with a personal, single-meal pilot. Add three meals, priced budgets and sharing only after their checks pass. Record which features each household used.

Weeks 11–12 — Learn from use
Fix pilot issues, report groups with a full 28 days of observation, and prepare the demo and startup evidence. A delayed pilot means a later report.

The initial pilot uses a catalog with verified content rights, one meal and questions about the selected recipe. Shared access, strict budgets and saved private recipe or photo uploads are outside that first release.

QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU START

Can I use the new features yet?
Sunny's text search and photo search with ingredient confirmation are available. A saved pantry, meal planner, shopping calculations, cook-along, household sharing and pilot dashboard are planned. The full demo journey depends on those features being built.

Does local AI mean my phone works offline?
The models run on a local Linux server, and your browser needs a connection to it. A fully offline phone experience is not implemented. Sunny's inference does not use cloud AI, but the website may still load ordinary web resources such as fonts.

What do the pilot and catalog figures mean?
The catalog size describes the technical test collection, not the number of customers. Pilot percentages are targets, not results or promises of savings. Retention, willingness to pay and revenue need evidence from actual use.

How are images, content and dietary needs handled?
The local catalog has recipe sources and original photos. Public demos need clear rights to the code, recipes and images; the proposed pilot uses 100 licensed or team-created recipes. Check recipes and ingredient labels when you have allergies. Image recognition and filters do not certify food safety.
